# CFB Player Props v2

CFB-PLAYER-v1 remains the independent projection base. v2 adds market-specific walk-forward calibration, probability/EV authority gates, confidence 0-100 and 1-5 stars, prospective signal freezing, settlement and CLV.

Stars must be empirical confidence labels. They may not be generated from projection-minus-line alone. A missing calibrated hit probability returns UNCALIBRATED and cannot receive authoritative stars.

Expansion order after validation: QB1/QB2 uncertainty; RB1/RB2 workload; WR1/WR2/WR3; TE1. Markets expand only when point-in-time history supports calibration. TD markets stay off until their discrete distributions validate.

Promotion requires sufficient OOS N, positive units/ROI after real economics, calibration, season stability, confidence monotonicity, and prospective evidence. No automatic wager authority is granted by this implementation.
