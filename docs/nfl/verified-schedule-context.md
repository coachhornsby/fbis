# Verified schedule context and injury integration

`attachVerifiedNflScheduleContext(game, checked, now)` consumes only the object returned by `validateVerifiedNflInput`. Call after `attachNflVerseFeatures` and before `attachNflProShadow`. It does not fetch, persist, or authorize wagers.

The complete season schedule plus completed-game receipt establish each side's last actual completed scheduled game. Rest is the difference between Eastern calendar dates of kickoffs, including DST transitions. A missing intervening NFL week establishes coming off a bye. A short week is less than seven calendar days. Opening-week or unverified previous-game context remains unknown. Existing NFL-PRO rest coefficients affect scores; no additional bye bonus is added, avoiding double counting. Coefficients are existing research assumptions, not newly calibrated evidence.

The schedule alone does not establish an itinerary. Away designation, neutral-site designation, and known international venue are context, not proof of miles flown or days spent traveling. The adapter leaves travel miles unknown and removes unverified carried-over rest/travel/time-zone inputs. No travel score adjustment is claimed. A future verified itinerary adapter needs source URL, observation timestamp before kickoff, actual origin/destination/arrival, and venue coordinates with provenance. Great-circle distance must be labeled a proxy rather than actual miles traveled. International context already exists in `nflInternationalContext.js` and must not assert true home advantage overseas.

## Injury source and model boundary

`nflOfficialInjuries.js` already parses official practice/game status tables from https://www.nfl.com/injuries/ and normalizes observations, but invokes storage writes. Do not call that ingestion inside this pure recovery consumer or bypass account-budget admission. An admitted offline publication should carry bounded current-game official observations and player identity/snap/depth records, with original report time, observation time, game/week identity and expiry. Current-season nflverse player histories alone do not establish availability.

`evaluateNflPersonnelResearch` in `nflPersonnelChallenger.js` is the existing pure availability consumer. It requires pre-kickoff baseline/capture timestamps; rejects future, stale, carried or unverified states; separates expected roster presence from confirmed game-day active status; and blocks inadequate roster coverage. Its coefficients are explicitly exploratory, `calibrationStatus: NOT_VALIDATED`, `changesProductionChampion: false`. Integrate its output as an injury-aware research scenario with HOLD/provisional flags and missingness, not as a silently promoted champion adjustment. Never label missing official observations as healthy. Practice DNP does not mean game-day OUT.

To change the main published score with injuries, validate point-in-time source linkage and evaluate injury effects out of sample (including replacement/position usage and uncertain statuses), version that model separately, and preserve original pregame freezes. Current NFL-PRO v1.2 explicitly states injury status is not consumed. Wiring an unvalidated shadow must not alter that claim or allow wagers. No live injury-source freshness or production deployment was verified by this adapter work.
