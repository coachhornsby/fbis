# FBIS Product Definition

FBIS is a proprietary sports forecasting and wagering-decision system.

Its competitive advantage is **not** sportsbook aggregation.

Its competitive advantage is:

- independent predictive models (PURE)
- calibrated uncertainty (when authority exists)
- model-vs-market comparison
- disciplined qualification
- risk control
- immutable decision evidence
- prospective validation (Model Lab)

Market providers are replaceable infrastructure.

PURE models are proprietary intellectual property.

## Primary Board questions

1. What does FBIS think?
2. What can I actually bet?
3. How different is FBIS from the market?
4. What should I do? (Research / Watch / Qualified / Authorized / Bet / Pass)

## Authority honesty

UI must reflect authority. UI cannot create authority.

- RESEARCH remains RESEARCH
- No probability / fair odds / EV / qualification / authorization unless the model contract supports it
- Model difference ≠ EV
- ACTION remains downstream market intelligence
- Pinnacle/reference is optional and never blocks the Board
